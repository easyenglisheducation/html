Anclaje Neural: Pasado Progresivo

Usamos el Pasado Progresivo para hablar de acciones que estaban ocurriendo en un momento específico del pasado. La clave neural es combinar el pasado del verbo To Be (WAS / WERE) con un verbo terminado en -ING.

Reglas de Ortografía (-ING)

  • Regla General: Agrega -ing (work ➔ working, play ➔ playing).
  • Termina en -e muda: Elimina la -e y pon -ing (drive ➔ driving, write ➔ writing).
  • CVC: Duplica la última consonante (run ➔ running, swim ➔ swimming).

Marcadores de Tiempo Clave

  • at 8:00 PM yesterday: "I was working at 8:00 PM yesterday."
  • while (mientras): "She was cooking while he was studying."
  • when (cuando): "We were watching TV when you called."
